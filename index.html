40,000+ titles

The Front
Room

Walk in and the front room hits you first: horror shirts on the rack, Halloween VHS in the case, Argento in 4K behind glass. New, rare, international, cult and horror cinema — over 40,000 titles on Blu-ray, DVD, 4K and tape, in the middle of Highland Park.

--:-- LA Open daily 11am–10pm

fresh on the floor

Music on
VHS

A dreamy set of music VHS hit the floor — Madonna, the Pretenders and more, straight out of the tape era. The shop runs on physical media: films to rent and buy, and a vinyl room's worth of records to dig through next to the tapes.

--:-- LA Open daily 11am–10pm

rentals & sales

Come Rent
Movies

The sign on Figueroa says it plainly: come rent movies. Rent the rare stuff, buy the keepers, sell us your stack. Keeping physical media alive in Los Angeles since 2003 — open every day, 11am to 10pm, for in-store browsing.

Privacy

Looking for a title?

Hunting a rare one? Ask us — the crew answers stock questions every day, and if it is on our shelves we will put it aside for you. Or just write to info@vidtheque.com.

Plan your visit

We are at 4102 N Figueroa St in Highland Park, open daily 11am–10pm. Ask about browsing, rentals, or a shop tour with Lucé — and say hello to Jules the shop cat.

Join the screening list

Screenings, Video Visions comedy nights and events in the microcinema — get on the list and we will tell you what is on, when. One night only means one night only.
